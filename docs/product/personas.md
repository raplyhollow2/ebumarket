# Personas

## 1. Maya — Teen seller / cleaner of closet

- **Age:** 16  
- **Need:** Sell a few tops and a jacket; wants it to feel safe and not scammy  
- **Behavior:** Phone-first; takes photos quickly; impatient with long forms  
- **Success:** Listing submitted in under ~5 minutes; understands “pending” means not live yet  
- **Fear:** Buyer no-shows; fake payment; awkward meetup  

## 2. Jordan — Teen buyer

- **Age:** 15  
- **Need:** Cheap, cute clothes near them; prefers verified listings  
- **Behavior:** Skims photos; checks price + meetup area; wants COD  
- **Success:** Finds an item, understands payment breakdown, requests meetup/checkout  
- **Fear:** Misrepresented condition; pressure to pay online  

## 3. Sam — Teen donor

- **Age:** 17  
- **Need:** Give away clothes that won’t sell; zero friction  
- **Behavior:** Will upload fewer details than a sale listing  
- **Success:** Donation listing live (or pending if we mirror verification); someone claims it  

## 4. Rivera Community Closet — Org claimer

- **Type:** Local youth org / school closet  
- **Need:** Browse donation hub; request batches of items  
- **Success:** Submit claim/request with contact + pickup preference  
- **Note:** MVP may use a single account type with an “organization” profile flag  

## 5. Alex — Zyra admin (team)

- **Need:** Queue of pending listings with photos; approve/reject; see transaction status  
- **Success:** Clear queue, one-tap verify, audit of what’s live vs pending  

## Shared teen constraints

- Short attention; large tap targets  
- Location privacy: general area + meetup points, not exact home address by default  
- Trust cues must be explicit (badge + short explanation)
